# Critical Journeys

## Find forgotten work

The five-minute local scan discovers genuine loose ends, orders interrupted or
oldest work first, renders bounded plain-text rows, and collapses the bar when
the queue is empty.

## Fail safely and ship honestly

Malformed, partial, or hostile input never becomes a false clean state. The
submission lane blocks until the exact source revision loads in Buzz, produces
a readable 16:9 preview, and receives approval bound to that preview hash.
